What we collect

  • Account details: the email address and password you sign up with. Your password is never stored in plain text – only a salted hash of it is kept.
  • Game progress: your coins, upgrades, prestige layers, achievements, and task completions, saved as a single game-state record tied to your account so you can pick up where you left off on any device.
  • Session cookie: a short-lived, signed authentication cookie that keeps you logged in. It contains no personal information beyond a reference to your account.

What we don't collect

We don't run advertising, and we don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. There are no third-party ad trackers on this site.

How we use it

Your account details and session cookie exist purely to let you log in and keep your save tied to you. Your game state is used only to render and restore your progress, including calculating offline progress when you come back. Basic, aggregate performance metrics (page load speed, not tied to your identity) may be collected to help us keep the game running smoothly.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and save data for as long as your account exists. If you'd like your account and all associated data deleted, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Security

Passwords are hashed before storage, authentication uses a signed token, and the connection between your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a request to delete your data? Reach out to the site owner directly through whichever channel they shared this game with you on.
